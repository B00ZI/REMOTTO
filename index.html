FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about pricing, licenses, or lesson materials? We’ve got answers.

How does billing work for schools?

Schools can subscribe via flexible monthly or annual invoicing. We also support official purchase orders (POs) and school-wide institutional licenses with multi-teacher management.

Can individual teachers subscribe?

Yes! Independent teachers can choose our Teacher Plan to get full, unlimited access to the complete A1–C1 curriculum, slide decks, audio dialogues, and printable worksheets.

What formats are the lesson materials in?

All lessons include PowerPoint (PPTX) and interactive PDF slide decks for screen sharing, print-ready PDF student worksheets, step-by-step teacher scripts, and MP4/MP3 audio dialogues.

Can I test lessons before purchasing?

Absolutely! You can explore free sample lessons across all proficiency levels (A1–C1) instantly on our website without needing any credit card or commitment.

Are materials suitable for online and in-person teaching?

Yes, Remtoo is fully optimized for both. The slide presentations work seamlessly on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and physical classroom interactive whiteboards.
